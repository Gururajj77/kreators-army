The files are the woff2 subsets (latin and latin-ext) that Google Fonts serves for the family, the same files kreators.app serves,
saved here so that no request goes to a third party when a page loads. They keep two of the family's axes, weight (1 to 1000)
and roundness (ROND, 0 to 100); no glyph is changed. The latin-ext file holds the rupee sign.
